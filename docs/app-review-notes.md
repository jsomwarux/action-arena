# App Review Notes

## Product Summary
Action Arena is a free-to-play fantasy sports prediction game with private and public leagues, weekly virtual coin budgets, matchups, season standings, and cosmetic progression.

## Why This Is Not Gambling
Action Arena has no real-money entry, no real-money cash out, and no in-app prize fulfillment. Coins, points, matchup scores, standings, and cosmetics are virtual and have no monetary value.

There is no consideration-prize-chance triangle inside the app. If a league has prizes, those are arranged and settled by the league commissioner outside Action Arena and are not facilitated by the app.

The app contains no real-money transactions related to gameplay, no external sportsbook links, and no facilitation of real-money gambling.

## Category Peers
The intended category peers are fantasy sports apps such as Sleeper, Yahoo Fantasy, and ESPN Fantasy: league-based competition with friends, weekly matchups, season standings, chat, history, and bragging rights.

## Review Path
1. Log in with the demo account.
2. Confirm the one-time "How Action Arena Works" disclosure if it appears.
3. Open Leagues and enter the demo league.
4. Open Pick Board.
5. Add straight, parlay, and teaser picks to the Lineup.
6. Review the Lineup and submit picks.
7. Open Pick History from Profile or the league screen.
8. Open a matchup detail screen to see submitted picks and virtual coin scoring.
9. Open Settings > About to view the permanent no-real-money disclosure and Terms summary.

## Reviewer Notes
Action Arena uses sports data and virtual values to score fantasy prediction leagues. No real money is wagered or won in Action Arena. The app is not affiliated with any sportsbook or gambling operator and contains no external sportsbook destination links.
