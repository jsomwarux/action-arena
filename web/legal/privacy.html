Action Arena Privacy Policy

Action Arena uses account, league, pick, notification, and cosmetic data to operate the app experience. We do not sell personal data, and no real-money sports operator receives app data from Action Arena.

Support Home

Action Arena is a free-to-play fantasy sports prediction game. Coins, points, standings, and cosmetics are virtual and have no monetary value. No real money is paid, won, or redeemable inside the app, and there are no redemption features or links to real-money sports operators.

Data We Collect

We collect only the data needed to create accounts, run leagues, show picks and standings, deliver optional notifications, and manage Season Pass access.

  • Contact Info: email address for Supabase email authentication, sign-in, account recovery, and account management.
  • Identifiers: your Supabase user ID and authentication session identifiers. If you enable notifications, we may store an Expo push notification token tied to your account so app notifications can be delivered.
  • Account and profile data: display name, avatar URL, Arena Coins balance, notification preferences, equipped cosmetics, and related account settings.
  • User Content: league names and descriptions you create, team names, league membership, chat messages, stickers, shared picks, content reports, blocked-user records, and gameplay content such as picks, bet legs, matchup results, standings, achievements, and season history.
  • Purchases: Season Pass entitlement status, season year, source, and Apple in-app purchase validation metadata such as product ID, transaction ID, original transaction ID, Apple purchase date, Apple receipt validation environment, and receipt validation time.
  • Gameplay data: league settings, invite codes, weekly cards, pick amounts, odds, selections, results, profits, standings, matchups, leaderboard information, cosmetics purchases using virtual Arena Coins, and notification events generated by app activity.

How We Use Data

  • To create, authenticate, recover, and manage your account.
  • To let you create and join leagues, submit picks, view matchups, chat with league members, and see standings, achievements, cosmetics, and profile stats.
  • To enforce gameplay rules, moderate objectionable content, process reports and blocks, and protect league integrity.
  • To deliver optional push notifications for league activity, pick reminders, results, awards, and similar app functionality.
  • To verify, restore, and maintain your Season Pass entitlement.
  • To troubleshoot app functionality and maintain service reliability.

Storage and Security

Action Arena stores account, league, pick, standings, notification, cosmetic, moderation, and Season Pass entitlement data in Supabase. Supabase provides authentication, database storage, row-level security, realtime updates, and server-side Edge Functions used by the app. Authentication sessions are stored on your device so you can stay signed in.

We use access controls so users can access the app data needed for their own account and leagues. No method of storage or transmission is perfectly secure, but we use Supabase security features and app-level authorization rules to limit access to personal data.

In-App Purchases

Apple processes payment for the Season Pass through Apple In-App Purchase. Action Arena does not receive your full payment card details. To unlock or restore the Season Pass, the app sends Apple receipt data and related transaction identifiers to a Supabase Edge Function, which validates the receipt with Apple and stores the resulting Season Pass entitlement in Supabase.

The Season Pass unlocks premium extras such as exclusive cosmetics, advanced analytics, early Pick Board access when configured, and future ad-free hooks. Core gameplay remains free, and Action Arena does not offer real-money wagering, cash-out, or redemption features.

Analytics and Crash Reporting

The current app does not include a third-party analytics SDK or crash-reporting SDK. Development builds include lightweight console logging for typed app events such as league creation, chat messages, cosmetic actions, and Season Pass actions. Those console logs are for development troubleshooting and are not used for advertising or third-party tracking.

Data Retention and Account Deletion

We retain account and gameplay data while your account is active so leagues, standings, picks, chat, notifications, cosmetics, and Season Pass entitlements can function. Some league history may remain where needed to preserve league records, enforce moderation decisions, or comply with legal, security, or operational requirements.

You can delete your account in the app by opening Settings and choosing Delete Account. The app sends an authenticated deletion request to Supabase, deletes the Supabase Auth user, signs you out locally, and clears local cached app data. Database records tied to your user ID are deleted or cleaned up according to the app's Supabase deletion rules, including account, league membership, pick, standings, notification, cosmetic, chat, and Season Pass records that are linked to the deleted user.

If you cannot access the app, contact us at [CONTACT EMAIL] and include the email address on the account you want deleted.

Children

Action Arena is not directed to children. Because the app is a simulated sports prediction game with a simulated-gambling age rating, it is intended for users old enough to use apps with that rating in their region. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children.

No Sale of Data, Ads, or Tracking

We do not sell personal data. We do not use personal data for advertising. We do not share app data with real-money sports operators. We do not use third-party tracking for advertising or cross-app tracking.
